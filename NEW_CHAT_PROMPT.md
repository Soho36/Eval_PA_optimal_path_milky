# First message for the new Codex chat

Begin by reading `AGENTS.md` and following the complete reading order in
`project_memory/BEGINNING_OF_A_WORK_SESSION.md`. Verify
`TRANSFER_MANIFEST.json` before importing or changing code.

This is the Legacy 25K “milky cow” study. The source strategy first creates a
global one-position completed-trade opportunity stream. Every opportunity
accepted for PA trading is copied simultaneously to every eligible active PA.
There is no staggering, no routing competition and no dormant PA inventory.
Accounts may later diverge through activation times, balances, payouts,
contract scaling and deaths.

Treat the copied rules, RR1 tape, upstream manifests and Evaluation parity
fixture as evidence. Treat payout policies and files under `reference/` only as
candidates or implementation references. Do not import the parent project's
`max_headroom` routing, K=2/S=1 conclusion, inventory results, fill-rate
mechanics or optimum claims.

First audit the rules and input contracts for the copy-to-all model. Then define
and test copy-to-all parity, scaling, replacement, acquisition and external-
capital contracts. Do not begin an integrated parameter sweep until those
contracts and fixtures are executable. The eventual study should compare 1-20
active Legacy 25K PAs, all six payout candidates, replacement/acquisition
policies and explicitly defined MNQ scaling thresholds under rolling cohorts
and regime/stress reporting.
