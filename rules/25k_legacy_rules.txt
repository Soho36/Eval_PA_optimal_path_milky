LEGACY 25K COUNTERFACTUAL STUDY RULES
Version: 2026-08-31.study-baseline.3
Product: Apex Legacy 25K Rithmic only
Status: user-authorized study specification; not a claim of historical firm availability

1. PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY

This file is the primary human-readable rule contract for the first integrated
Evaluation -> PA -> withdrawal/reinvestment study. It applies to every simulated
instant and purchase cohort unless a named sensitivity scenario overrides a
field. The half-open applicability interval is [-infinity, +infinity).

The study is intentionally counterfactual: a new Legacy 25K Evaluation is
available for purchase at every simulated timestamp. Actual product retirement,
offer gaps, and account grandfathering are ignored. No other account type or
platform is permitted in this baseline.

Rule-status labels used below:

- USER_STUDY_ASSUMPTION: explicitly selected for this research question.
- SUPPLIED_RULE_EXCERPT: supported by EV-account_rules.txt or Payout_rules.txt,
  but not treated as a dated historical representation.
- UPSTREAM_COMPATIBILITY_ASSUMPTION: selected to reproduce or conservatively
  extend pinned upstream behavior.
- OUT_OF_SCOPE: intentionally absent from the first model.

Machine-readable companions:

- config/legacy_25k_baseline.json
- config/payout_policies.json

2. TIME AND SESSION INTERPRETATION

- Interpret every source timestamp as a Europe/Tallinn local wall-clock label
  and attach the IANA Europe/Tallinn timezone. Apply its historical DST rules.
- If a source label is ambiguous during the autumn DST fold, choose fold=0
  (the first occurrence). Reject nonexistent spring-gap wall times rather than
  silently shifting them.
- The modeled session opens at 01:00 and closes at 23:59 local time on the same
  local calendar date. That local date is the trading-day identifier.
- The completed-trade input is authoritative for entry and exit labels. The
  model does not convert the strategy to Eastern Time and does not model holiday
  or early-close exceptions.
- The MQL5 example declares no source timezone and defaults its flatten control
  to 23:30. It does not establish the study clock. Europe/Tallinn and the
  01:00/23:59 boundaries are the user's superseding study convention.
- Session open/close drives cohort, trading-day, and payout decisions; it is not
  a filter that deletes authoritative completed fills. Retain the two observed
  00:00 entries, 11 observed 00:00-00:59 exits, and 97 trades whose exit local
  date differs from their entry local date. Evaluation trading-day credit uses
  the entry local calendar date; PA realized P&L and profitable-day credit use
  the exit local calendar date.
- An existing position occupying [entry_at, exit_at) is free at exit_at. Thus an
  exit at T is processed before a possible entry at T.

Status: USER_STUDY_ASSUMPTION.

3. HISTORICAL START COHORTS AND CAPITAL

- Generate rolling historical start cohorts from valid session starts in the
  normalized trade tape. Each cohort is a separate historical starting
  opportunity, not another simultaneous customer.
- Each cohort begins with zero Evaluations, zero PAs, and treasury/external
  capital sufficient to buy one Evaluation. Buy exactly one Evaluation at the
  cohort start. The first purchase seeds the fixed-cadence clock.
- The initial seed contribution equals the applicable Evaluation purchase fee.
  Until the first PA is activated, contribute only any shortfall needed for
  scheduled Evaluation renewals and that first PA activation, just in time, and
  record every top-up as external capital. After the first PA exists, later
  Evaluation purchases and renewals must use treasury cash and receive no
  automatic external top-ups.
- This convention is named `through_first_pa` and remains the default baseline.
- The named `through_inventory_target` sensitivity uses the same initial state,
  but may buy later Evaluations when cadence and the running-Evaluation cap
  permit even before the first PA exists. Spend treasury cash first, then
  contribute only the just-in-time shortfall for base-stock purchases, renewals,
  and activations. The bridge closes permanently when simultaneous live,
  activated PA inventory first reaches K active plus S standby PAs. Later PA
  deaths never reopen it. Every contribution remains visible as external capital.
- A future existing-subscription starting scenario is separate and is not part
  of this baseline.

Status: USER_STUDY_ASSUMPTION. `through_first_pa` is the main starting-capital
convention. `through_inventory_target` is a user-authorized, separately reported
external-inventory-building sensitivity selected 2026-08-31; the two modes must
not be pooled as though they had the same capital constraint.

4. COMMERCIAL TERMS

- Evaluation purchase/30-calendar-day renewal fee: USD 35.
- PA activation fee: USD 125.
- New Evaluations are perpetually available at USD 35.
- A live Evaluation renews at each fixed 30-calendar-day boundary and carries
  its balance, peak and drawdown state. A failed Evaluation remains dormant until
  that boundary, then renews into a fresh Evaluation. The renewal is charged once
  and its one-position adapter restarts.
- At an exact boundary, a trade exit and its pass/failure decision occur before
  renewal processing.

Status: availability is USER_STUDY_ASSUMPTION; amounts and fixed-cycle behavior
are UPSTREAM_COMPATIBILITY_ASSUMPTION values from the pinned EODMAE candidate.
They are parameters, not claims about all historical Apex prices.

5. EVALUATION ACCOUNT

Identity and economics:

- Starting balance: USD 25,000.
- Profit target: USD 1,500; target balance: USD 26,500.
- Trailing drawdown amount: USD 1,500; initial liquidation threshold: USD 23,500.
- Daily loss limit: none.
- Position size: 3 MNQ per accepted Evaluation trade. This is an inherited
  candidate to test, not an integrated optimum.
- Execution baseline: USD 1.05 round-turn commission per MNQ and zero slippage;
  named sensitivity scenarios may change execution costs without changing rules.

Opportunity adapter:

- Replay all 23 strategy windows in one causal stream for each Evaluation and
  each renewal cycle.
- The Evaluation has one global position slot. A raw offer is accepted only if
  that slot is free at the offer's entry time. Any offer arriving while the slot
  is occupied is blocked for that Evaluation.
- The completed trade's [entry_at, exit_at) interval is a filled-position-time
  proxy. Pending orders, fill probability, re-pricing, and broker/order lifecycle
  events are not modeled.
- This exactly matches the pinned EODMAE fill-time phase adapter and is only a
  semantic approximation of the supplied source strategy. The supplied
  `RR_r_MFE_buy-stop-entry(EXAMPLE).cs` (MQL5 despite its `.cs` suffix) checks
  the one-position condition when a new setup bar is processed, but its CSV
  exposes only later fill/exit times, not setup or pending-order times. Exact
  source-EA parity is therefore impossible from this completed-trade tape. The
  Evaluation adapter intentionally differs from the PA opportunity adapter.

Drawdown and pass:

- Update the Evaluation trailing threshold from the highest modeled intratrade
  equity supported by the completed trade's MFE/MAE path scenario. The threshold
  is peak equity minus USD 1,500.
- Do not freeze the Evaluation threshold. This is the stricter pinned-EODMAE
  compatibility assumption; it is explicitly configurable for sensitivity.
- Touching the threshold fails the Evaluation: modeled equity <= threshold is a
  failure. This conservative boundary matches pinned upstream behavior even
  though the supplied prose uses "below."
- Test drawdown survival before crediting a pass at the same trade event. A pass
  requires realized net balance >= USD 26,500 and at least one completed trading
  day.
- Require one accepted Evaluation trading day for the entire baseline interval.
  The user confirms on 2026-08-30 that the one-day pass has been the operating
  reality since roughly 2025-09 and that the seven-day figure in
  EV-account_rules.txt survives only in tutorial text. The value is therefore a
  confirmed current rule, not a guess.
  It remains counterfactual over most of this tape, which begins 2020-01-02, so
  roughly the first five and a half years are simulated under a rule that did
  not yet apply to them. The divergence is large: at seven days the same 100
  starts over 180 days fall from 21 to 11 cohorts reaching a payout and from
  $14,000 to $5,500 in gross payouts. Keep it replaceable by a dated scenario.
  Evaluation day credit uses the accepted trade's entry local calendar date,
  including the explicitly retained 00:00 completed fills.

Status: account values/no daily loss are SUPPLIED_RULE_EXCERPT or pinned 25K
inputs; 3 MNQ is an inherited candidate; opportunity, equality, no-freeze, and
one-day promotion behavior are labeled study/compatibility assumptions.

6. EVALUATION PASS AND PA ACTIVATION

- A qualifying Evaluation passes at the exit timestamp of the qualifying trade.
- Charge the USD 125 activation fee once and activate the resulting PA at that
  same timestamp. There is no review, purchase-link, setup, or tradability delay
  in the baseline.
- A PA born at T is activated immediately but is not eligible for a PA signal
  whose entry timestamp is also T. It becomes tradable for entries strictly
  after T. The event ordering must be deterministic and recorded in the run
  manifest.

Status: immediate paid activation is UPSTREAM_COMPATIBILITY_ASSUMPTION.

7. PERFORMANCE ACCOUNT AND ROUTING

Account mechanics:

- PA starting balance: USD 25,000.
- Trailing drawdown amount: USD 1,500; initial threshold: USD 23,500.
- Trail the threshold USD 1,500 below the modeled intratrade equity peak until
  it reaches USD 25,100. It never rises above USD 25,100.
- Touching the threshold kills the PA: modeled equity <= threshold is failure.
  A failed PA cannot be reset.
- Each selected PA trades exactly one MNQ for a signal copy.

Opportunity and exposure contract:

- Retain all raw concurrent cross-window opportunities in the PA phase.
- Each signal requests a fixed number R of one-MNQ copies. The baseline R is 1.
  K is the target number of active routed PA drawdown containers and is a
  separate decision variable. Changing K must never change requested R.
- S is the target number of already activated standby PAs. A standby is dormant:
  it receives no signals, accrues no trading or payout days, and cannot request a
  payout. When an active PA dies, promote the oldest standby by activation time
  and then PA ID before processing an entry at that timestamp. A promoted PA
  remains active; there is no later demotion.
- A newly activated PA fills an unoccupied active slot first and otherwise enters
  standby. Every live PA has exactly one role. Base-stock inventory position is
  live active plus live standby PAs, running Evaluations, and pending activations;
  it may not exceed K + S.
- `max_concurrent_evaluations`, when set, limits running Evaluation subscriptions
  only. A passed Evaluation pending activation counts toward base stock but is not
  running. The default is unbounded for backward compatibility; the inventory
  grid explicitly compares one and two.
- Route copies only to alive, tradable, non-busy PAs. Use max_headroom ordering:
  greatest (equity - liquidation_threshold), then fewer prior assignments, then
  lower monotonically allocated PA ID.
- Positions occupy [entry_at, exit_at). An existing PA exiting at T is eligible
  for an entry at T; a newly activated/replacement PA at T is not eligible for
  an entry at T.
- K >= 5R is an inherited historical capacity candidate for this exact tape,
  not a rule or guaranteed future bound. Compare K policies while holding R
  fixed and report requested versus delivered exposure.
- Do not impose a separate firm-wide PA ownership or activation cap in the
  baseline. K is the explicit study variable; a cap can be added only as a named
  sensitivity scenario. The supplied excerpts do not define such a cap.

Compliance scope:

- Contract scaling limits are nonbinding because a selected PA receives one MNQ.
- The fixed strategy uses 1:1 risk/reward and therefore satisfies a 5:1 maximum
  risk-to-reward restriction.
- DCA, adding, hedging, and variable sizing are inapplicable unless completed-
  trade evidence contradicts the fixed strategy contract.
- The 30% MAE/compliance guideline is OUT_OF_SCOPE. Do not create failure,
  warning, probation, or eligibility events from it.

8. PAYOUT ELIGIBILITY AND AMOUNT LIMITS

Apply payout state independently to each PA.

- A request opportunity requires at least eight completed PA trading days since
  PA activation (first request) or the last executed request (later requests).
- At least five days in that window must have realized net profit >= USD 50.
- PA balance must be at least USD 26,600 at the request decision.
- Gross request minimum: USD 500.
- Gross request cap: USD 1,500 for executed payouts 1 through 5; no fixed dollar
  cap from payout 6 onward.
- For payouts 1 through 3, the greatest amount allowed by the Safety Net is
  USD 500 + max(0, balance - USD 26,600). For payout 4 onward, the Safety Net is
  treated as ended and the greatest amount allowed by balance is
  max(0, balance - USD 25,100.01). The one-cent margin is required because
  threshold touch fails. Apply the payout-number cap after this formula. This
  post-third formula is an explicit configurable study interpretation.
- The 30% payout consistency rule is OUT_OF_SCOPE and never blocks a request.
- Payout request, approval, PA balance removal, and treasury receipt are one
  atomic event at the decision timestamp. There are no delays or denials.
- The trader receives 100% of the first USD 25,000 of cumulative gross approved
  payouts per PA and 90% thereafter. The PA balance is reduced by the gross
  request; treasury receives the split-adjusted amount.

9. PAYOUT POLICY COMPARISON

Compare exactly these six candidate policies from config/payout_policies.json
(schema v2):

1. minimum_500_always      - the USD 500 minimum whenever eligible.
2. maximum_always          - the maximum eligible gross whenever eligible.
3. rush_to_uncapped        - USD 500 for payouts 1-5, then maximum eligible.
4. cap_maximizer           - the full USD 1,500 for payouts 1-5, waiting until
                             that whole amount is eligible, then maximum.
5. preserve_safety_net     - maximum eligible, but never leaving the balance
                             below USD 26,600.
6. half_of_maximum         - 50% of the eligible maximum, floored at USD 500.

The axis under test is the payout counter, not a dollar trigger. The USD 1,500
cap ends after payout 5 and the Safety Net after payout 3, so the number of
early requests is itself a decision: five minimum requests reach the uncapped
regime in the fewest eligible windows, while waiting for the full cap extracts
more per window but stays capped longer. preserve_safety_net supplies the third
pole by refusing to encroach on the balance gate at all.

A policy chooses an amount only inside the section 8 eligible range. Each is an
early rule, an optional switch after a payout number, and an optional late
rule. A rule is minimum, maximum, fixed or fraction. A fixed rule with
require_full waits for its whole amount rather than silently degrading into a
smaller policy. An optional post-payout balance floor may cap any policy and is
re-checked at execution.

This set supersedes the six accumulation-trigger policies of version
2026-08-29.study-baseline.1, which descended from the Accounts_staggering
USD 200 per USD 400 lifetime-gain ratchet. Two of those were measurably
identical because the eight-trading-day and USD 26,600 gates always bound
first. The accumulator mechanism is retained in code and tests so that set
remains reproducible as a named sensitivity.

None of the six is an integrated optimum until this repository compares them
under common inputs at fixed R.

9A. TREASURY PURCHASE TIMING (STAGGERING)

The number of active and standby PAs achieved is an outcome of when cash is
spent, not an input. A container costs USD 35 per Evaluation cycle plus USD 125
activation and arrives only when that Evaluation passes, a median 61 days after
the decision to buy it. K active plus S standby is a base-stock upper bound for
every policy below and is never a level reached on demand.

1. greedy_to_target   - buy whenever cash allows and the pipeline is below K.
2. fixed_cadence      - at most one Evaluation per cadence_days, measured from
                        the previous successful purchase and seeded at the
                        cohort start. A failed/no-cash attempt does not move the
                        clock, and a PA death does not bypass cadence.
3. death_replacement  - buy only against accumulated PA deaths; never grow the
                        book beyond the size it already reached.
4. headroom_triggered - buy when the least-cushioned live PA falls below
                        headroom_trigger_usd, or when no PA is alive.
5. cash_buffer        - greedy, but never spend below cash_reserve_usd, so an
                        existing renewal or pending activation is not stranded.

These policies are not independent of section 9. A PA that only gains holds
headroom equal to its drawdown; it is the withdrawal that cuts the cushion,
because the floor has already frozen at plus USD 100 and a payout removes equity
above it. The payout policy therefore partly determines when a
headroom_triggered purchase fires. Do not tune the two families in isolation.

The inherited K >= 5R boundary is not carried in as a target. It answers a
capacity question under Accounts_staggering's premises, where a seat is already
owned, costs USD 200 and appears instantly. Peak positive-duration overlap on
this tape is five and that remains true, but it is the wrong question when a
container must be earned.

The 2026-08-31 inventory grid fixes R = 1 and crosses K = 1, 2, 3; S = 0, 1, 2;
cadence = 30, 45, 60, 90 days; one or two running Evaluations; both named
bootstrap modes; and all six payout candidates in section 9. The separate
copied-to-all-accounts "milky cow" design is not part of this project.

Status: USER_STUDY_ASSUMPTION, expanded 2026-08-31.

9B. STUDY HORIZON

The primary policy-comparison horizon is 720 days. 180 days is retained only as
a time-to-first-cash diagnostic.

180 days was inherited from EODMAE, whose episode ends at first paid activation.
Here the median cohort needs 61 days to reach a PA and 141 to a first payout, so
a 180-day horizon ends at roughly the first withdrawal and measures acquisition
rather than the reinvestment loop. On identical starts, moving from 180 to 540
days took gross payouts from USD 14,000 to USD 100,000 and flipped net realized
cash from minus USD 15,895 to plus USD 18,230. The tape spans 2,384 days, so 720
days still leaves roughly 1,100 fully observed rolling starts.

Status: USER_STUDY_ASSUMPTION, selected 2026-08-30.

10. INTENTIONALLY OMITTED EVENTS

The first baseline does not model:

- real-world Legacy product retirement or intermittent offers;
- account types other than Legacy 25K Rithmic;
- NT8/MT5 pending orders, re-pricing, fill mechanics, or broker rejection;
- Evaluation review delay or PA setup delay;
- PA 30% MAE/compliance enforcement;
- payout 30% consistency;
- payout request review, denial, processing delay, bank delay, or cancellation;
- DCA, adding, hedging, variable sizing, Live transfer, or unrelated conduct
  rules when absent from the completed-trade data.

These omissions define the baseline study boundary. They do not invalidate the
baseline, but a result must carry this rule-version identifier and must not be
described as exact historical Apex account performance.
